DEBT COLLECTION COMPLIANCE GUIDE

NCA (National Credit Act) Requirements
========================================

The National Credit Act regulates debt collection practices in South Africa. Key requirements:

1. Disclosure Requirements
- Debt collectors must identify themselves and their agency
- Must provide the debtor with written notice of the debt amount
- Must disclose the original creditor's name
- Must provide a breakdown of principal, interest, and fees

2. Communication Restrictions
- Calls may only be made between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM on weekdays
- Calls may only be made between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM on Saturdays
- No calls on Sundays or public holidays
- Maximum of 3 calls per day to the same debtor
- Maximum of 10 calls per week to the same debtor

3. Prohibited Practices
- Threatening legal action without intent to proceed
- Impersonating legal officials or law enforcement
- Misrepresenting the amount owed
- Adding unauthorized fees or charges
- Harassment or intimidation tactics
- Contacting third parties about the debt (except for tracing purposes)

POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) Consent Rules
===============================================================

POPIA governs the processing of personal information in South Africa:

1. Consent Requirements
- Must obtain explicit consent before processing personal information
- Consent must be specific, informed, and freely given
- Must allow debtors to withdraw consent at any time
- Must keep records of consent obtained

2. Data Protection
- Personal information must be processed lawfully and fairly
- Must only collect information necessary for the purpose
- Must implement adequate security measures
- Must retain information only as long as necessary
- Must allow debtors to access their information on request

3. Communication Consent
- Must record consent for SMS communications
- Must record consent for email communications
- Must record consent for automated calls
- Must respect opt-out requests immediately

Forbidden Phrases and Tactics
=============================

The following phrases and tactics are strictly prohibited:

1. Threatening Language
- "We will have you arrested"
- "The police are on their way"
- "You will go to jail for this"
- "We will seize your property immediately"
- "You will lose your job"

2. Misleading Statements
- "This is your final notice" (when it's not)
- "We are taking legal action today" (without intent)
- "Your credit score will be destroyed" (exaggeration)
- "We can garnish your wages" (without court order)
- "This will affect your immigration status" (irrelevant threats)

3. Harassment Tactics
- Calling repeatedly in short succession
- Calling at unreasonable hours
- Using profanity or abusive language
- Shouting or yelling at debtors
- Interrupting the debtor repeatedly

4. Privacy Violations
- Discussing the debt with family members
- Leaving messages with third parties
- Posting about the debt on social media
- Contacting the debtor's employer (except for verification)
- Threatening to contact the debtor's employer

Required Disclosures
===================

Every debt collection call must include:

1. Initial Disclosure
- "My name is [name] calling from [agency name]"
- "I am calling regarding an outstanding debt"
- "The original creditor is [creditor name]"
- "The current balance is [amount]"

2. Rights Disclosure
- "You have the right to request written verification of this debt"
- "You have the right to dispute this debt within 30 days"
- "You have the right to request payment arrangements"
- "You have the right to file a complaint with the NCR"

3. Privacy Disclosure
- "Your call may be recorded for quality and training purposes"
- "Your information is protected under POPIA"
- "We will not share your information with unauthorized parties"

Documentation Requirements
=========================

All debt collection activities must be documented:

1. Call Records
- Date and time of call
- Duration of call
- Number dialed
- Agent name and ID
- Outcome of call
- Next action required

2. Consent Records
- Date consent obtained
- Method of consent (phone, email, SMS)
- Specific consent given
- Any withdrawal of consent

3. Complaint Records
- Date of complaint
- Nature of complaint
- Resolution steps taken
- Final outcome
- Follow-up actions
