DEBT COLLECTION SCORECARD RUBRICS

C01: Professional Conduct
Score 1-5 based on:
- Maintains professional tone throughout call
- Uses appropriate language and avoids aggressive behavior
- Demonstrates empathy while maintaining firm boundaries
- Follows established communication protocols
- Handles difficult situations with composure

Rubric:
5: Exceeds expectations - Professional at all times, handles extreme situations gracefully
4: Meets expectations - Professional in most interactions, minor lapses
3: Acceptable - Generally professional but needs improvement in stress situations
2: Below expectations - Frequent unprofessional behavior or tone
1: Unacceptable - Consistently unprofessional, aggressive, or inappropriate

C02: Legal Compliance
Score 1-5 based on:
- Adheres to NCA regulations at all times
- Follows POPIA consent requirements
- Avoids forbidden phrases and tactics
- Provides required disclosures accurately
- Documents compliance properly

Rubric:
5: Exceeds expectations - Perfect compliance record, proactively identifies issues
4: Meets expectations - Full compliance with all regulations
3: Acceptable - Minor compliance issues, corrected promptly
2: Below expectations - Multiple compliance violations
1: Unacceptable - Severe or repeated legal violations

C03: Communication Effectiveness
Score 1-5 based on:
- Clearly explains debt details and payment options
- Listens actively to debtor concerns
- Provides accurate information consistently
- Follows up appropriately on agreements
- Maintains clear documentation of communications

Rubric:
5: Exceeds expectations - Exceptional clarity, resolves complex issues effectively
4: Meets expectations - Clear communication, good information flow
3: Acceptable - Generally clear but some confusion or repetition needed
2: Below expectations - Poor communication, frequent misunderstandings
1: Unacceptable - Ineffective communication, misleading information

C04: Problem Resolution
Score 1-5 based on:
- Identifies root causes of payment issues
- Offers appropriate solutions based on debtor situation
- Negotiates realistic payment arrangements
- Escalates complex cases appropriately
- Achieves sustainable resolutions

Rubric:
5: Exceeds expectations - Solves complex problems, creates lasting solutions
4: Meets expectations - Effective problem solving, good resolution rate
3: Acceptable - Adequate problem solving, some recurring issues
2: Below expectations - Poor problem solving, inadequate solutions
1: Unacceptable - Fails to resolve problems, creates new issues

C05: Documentation Quality
Score 1-5 based on:
- Completes all required documentation accurately
- Records call details thoroughly
- Updates system promptly
- Maintains organized records
- Ensures audit trail completeness

Rubric:
5: Exceeds expectations - Exceptional documentation, goes beyond requirements
4: Meets expectations - Complete and accurate documentation
3: Acceptable - Documentation mostly complete with minor errors
2: Below expectations - Incomplete or inaccurate documentation
1: Unacceptable - Critical documentation failures or missing records
